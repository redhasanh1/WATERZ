Frequently Asked Questions

We support MP4, MOV, and WebM video formats. Most videos from phones, cameras, and screen recordings work perfectly.

Videos can be up to 5 minutes long for free users. Premium users can process videos up to 30 minutes. File size limit is 100MB.

WebM and MOV (ProRes 4444) formats support alpha transparency. MP4 does not support transparency, so a solid background will be applied.

We use state-of-the-art AI models (SAM2) that automatically detect and segment the main subject in your video, separating it from the background frame by frame.
